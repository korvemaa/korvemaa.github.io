I had my MX-5 for a few years and loved pretty much everything about it. Manual, rear-wheel drive, tiny and completely impractical. Went to many track
days, did many first things in it and adored every minute I was in it.

Wasnt driving it much since I lived so close to town and it was getting expensive to keep, eventually circumstances meant I had to sell it.

I still miss it, whenever I see another mx5 around I cry a little.
